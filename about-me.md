## In my own words
I'm a Product Designer passionate about creating exceptional user experiences for everybody. Using principles of usable, meaningful, and inclusive design, I solve problems for a wider range of people — which leads to better business outcomes. With 10+ years of product design experience I bring together user-centred design, interaction design, and visual design, combined with a collaborative mindset and a deep interest in human psychology and behaviour. I'm interested in companies that place people at the centre of everything they do, value their employees' continued growth, and inspire by what they want to achieve.
