# Job Criteria — Scoring Rubric

How the agent scores every role. Hard filters reject outright; everything else scores 0–100. Tiers: 🔥 strong fit (75+), ⏳ maybe (50–74), discard (<50). Borderline roles go to "maybe", never silently dropped.

## Hard filters (reject immediately)
- Industry: gambling/betting, crypto/web3, tobacco/vaping/alcohol, surveillance/defence
- Level below Senior (no mid-weight/junior roles)
- Location: not Melbourne-commutable AND not AU-timezone remote
- Onsite requirement above 3 days/week
- Contract rate explicitly below $900/day (missing rate ≠ reject; note it)
- Perm salary explicitly below $140k base (missing salary ≠ reject; note it)

## Role type (weighting)
- Contract roles: scored normally
- Perm roles: apply a −10 handicap — perm must be exceptional to surface in 🔥 tier
- Contract length: 3–6 months ideal (+5); 6–12 fine; <4 weeks (−10)

## Level
- Accept: Senior Product Designer, Staff, Lead
- Reject: below senior
- Principal/Head-of: log in "maybe" with a note, don't score as fits

## Scoring signals — positive
| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| End-to-end product design ownership (discovery → delivery) | +15 |
| Discovery/exploration explicitly part of the role | +15 |
| 0→1 / greenfield / new product | +15 |
| AI-native product (agents, copilots, AI interaction patterns) | +15 |
| Evidence of craft/quality bar (design systems, polish, detail in the ad itself) | +10 |
| Startup (<50) or scale-up (50–500) | +10 |
| Domain: B2B SaaS/platforms, health, gov, education, public good | +10 |
| Domain overlap with my background (mental health, biometric verification, HR, gov) | +10 |
| Designer can clearly make visible impact (small design team, broad remit done right) | +10 |
| Melbourne hybrid ≤3 days or AU-timezone remote | +5 |

## Scoring signals — negative (red flags)
| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Ticket factory language ("work with PMs to deliver mockups", execution-only, no discovery) | −20 |
| Unicorn ad (UX + UI + research + front-end + marketing in one role) | −20 |
| Speed-worship ("fast-paced" as the lead virtue, "move fast", "thrive under pressure", aggressive deadlines) | −15 |
| Design-immature signals (design reports to eng/marketing, no design leadership, "no time for research") | −10 |
| Agency/consultancy or enterprise (not preferred, not banned) | −5 |
| Vague ad with no detail about the team or problem space | −5 |

## Judgment notes for the agent
- An ad scoring 🔥 must have at least one of: discovery/exploration, 0→1, AI-native, OR exceptional honest craft-led framing in a strong-fit domain (see calibration). Generic craft signals alone aren't enough.
- **Honest-ad bonus (+10):** ads that clearly state scope, what the role *is not*, who it's *not* for, and/or a salary range. This is one of Daniel's strongest positive signals.
- **Craft-led roles that de-emphasise formal research are still fits** if the ad is honest, substantive, and the domain matches. Do NOT apply the "no time for research" / design-immature penalty just because an ad says "defend a decision without user testing" or "not a standard UX-research role" — that's a deliberate craft stance, not immaturity. Penalise only genuine ticket-factory / execution-only signals.
- "Fast-paced" appearing once in boilerplate ≠ speed-worship; penalise when speed is framed as the culture.
- Missing rate/salary: keep the role, flag "rate unknown" prominently.
- Start date: Daniel is available ~9 Sep 2026. Roles needing an earlier start are NOT rejected — keep them and flag "starts before availability".
- Expired roles: score the same way; 🔥 and ⏳ expired roles both go to the outreach-targets section with named contacts.
- When unsure between tiers, choose the lower tier and say why in one line.

## Calibration set (Daniel's worked examples — highest-value input)
These three "would apply" ads define the target. Match new roles against their *spirit*, not just keywords.

### Would apply
- **Amber — Staff/Principal Product Designer (energy/renewables, Melbourne, $180–240k + options).** Why: lays out exactly what's involved, senior IC craft + ambiguity + cross-functional leadership, purpose-driven domain, salary range stated. *Textbook 🔥.*
- **Heidi Health — Product Designer (clinical AI, Sydney/Melbourne).** Why: brutally honest "if you answer no to these, this isn't for you" section; AI-native workflow expected; obsessive craft; meaningful health domain. De-emphasises formal research on purpose — still a strong fit.
- **Cadmus — Senior Product Designer (edtech/assessment, Melbourne hybrid 2 days).** Why: explicit "what this role is NOT" section; high visual craft + systems thinking; education/public-good domain; design framed as the mechanism of trust.

Common thread: honest, detailed, craft-led ads in people-centred/public-good domains, AU-based, senior IC scope, often with a salary range.

### Would never
- **Binance — Product Designer (crypto exchange).** Why: crypto (hard filter), "results-driven workplace", generic boilerplate responsibilities, Mandarin required.
- **xAI — Member of Technical Staff (X Core Product).** Why: "work ethic" + flat-structure language signalling long hours; actually an engineering role; US-based.
- **Crypto job ("we play to win", San Francisco).** Why: crypto (hard filter), "play to win" hustle-culture language, not AU.

Turn-off phrases to weight negatively: "results-driven", "we play to win", "work ethic", "hustle", crypto/web3 anything, generic responsibilities with no problem detail.
